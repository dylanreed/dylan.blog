# Muppet Monday Mornings - Quote Bank

A collection of opening quotes for the weekly Muppet appreciation series. Pick your favorite for each character!

---

## WEMBLEY FRAGGLE
*Fraggle Rock's most lovably indecisive friend*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| "What if I'm wrong? But what if I'm right? But what if I'm wrong?" | Fraggle Rock |

**Note:** Wembley's name comes from British slang "wemble" meaning to be indecisive.

## MARVIN SUGGS
*The demented Muppaphone maestro*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| "Of course they are alive! You cannot make music by hitting dead creatures!" | The Muppet Show|

**Note:** Frank Oz based the accent on his French friend. He imagined Marvin living in a scuzzy trailer park, keeping the Muppaphones in a cage.

---

## THE FLYING ZUCCHINI BROTHERS
*Italian daredevil acrobats*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| "It's a human cannonball act, The Zucchini Brothers!" | The Muppet Show |


**Note:** They hurl themselves from 200 feet to land in a bucket of water. Beauregard often accidentally moves the bucket.

## FOO-FOO
*Miss Piggy's pampered poodle*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| "Ruff! Ruff! Ruff! Ruff!" | (in response to "What's two plus two?") |

**Note:** Miss Piggy later "re-homed" Foo-Foo because the dog was "prejudiced against green people" and kept nipping at Kermit.

---

## MANOLO AND CARLO FLAMINGO
*Pink dancing duo*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| *[Performs the Macarena with Miss Piggy]* | Muppets Most Wanted |

**Note:** A pair of flamingo dancers who perform "Macarena" with Miss Piggy. Manolo is voiced by Peter Linz. Their puppets were later reused as Fred on Sesame Street.

---

## ANGEL MARIE
*The big dumb lovable pirate monster*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| "Aye, Aye!! You can have everyone spank me and call me names!… Or not." | Muppet Treasure Island |

**Note:** First appeared in Muppet Treasure Island as one of Long John Silver's crew. Later showed up as a flying monkey in Muppets' Wizard of Oz and a gulag chef in Muppets Most Wanted.

## THE AFGHAN HOUND
*The elegant blonde model dog*

| Quote | Source |
|-------|--------|
| *[Orders pizza during telethon when supposed to be taking pledges]* | The Muppets (2011) |

**Note:** Blond female dog with a long pointy snout who appeared from Season 2 onwards. Often in background of sketches. No consistent performer - has been played by Louise Gold, Richard Hunt, Kathryn Mullen, and Alice Dinnean.
